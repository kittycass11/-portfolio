01 — About

A little about me.

I'm Cass — 22, and I train out of DJL. This is my corner of the internet: honest, unfinished, and always in progress.

I'm not here to perform a perfect version of myself. I'd rather show the real thing — the early starts, the reps that don't feel good, the days I show up anyway. Training taught me that progress is quiet and mostly unglamorous. That's the part I've come to love.

Outside of training I'm curious about almost everything: design, music, the small details most people scroll past. I like clean lines, honest work, and people who mean what they say. If that sounds like you, my inbox is always open.
